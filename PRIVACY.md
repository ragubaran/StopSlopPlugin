# Privacy Policy for StopSlop

**Last updated:** September 7, 2026

StopSlop ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how StopSlop handles user data.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
StopSlop does **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or browsing history. All operations, detection heuristics, and filtering occur entirely on your local device within your browser.

## 2. Local Storage Usage
The extension uses Chrome's `storage.local` API strictly to save:
- Your user preferences (e.g., filter mode, sensitivity slider, category toggles).
- Your custom channel blocklist and keywords.
- A local list of recently flagged video titles (stored strictly on your machine).

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any external server or third party.

## 3. Host Permissions
StopSlop requests host access only for `https://*.youtube.com/*` in order to detect video cards in the DOM, evaluate title text locally, and apply visual filter styles (blurring or hiding). It does not access, monitor, or read data from any other website.

## 4. Third-Party Services
StopSlop does not use any third-party tracking, telemetry, or analytics services.
